# Fundraising Clarity Bubble

A free mini fundraising calculator that helps teams, schools, churches, booster clubs, nonprofits, families, and community groups see their fundraising goal clearly before the stress takes over.

This tool is designed to create a “clarity bubble” around the fundraiser — a simple moment where the goal stops feeling impossible and starts feeling reachable.

## What It Does

The user enters:

- Organization or team name
- Fundraising goal
- Amount already raised
- Days left
- Average donation amount
- Fundraiser type
- What the money will make possible

Then the app calculates:

- Amount still needed
- Daily target
- Weekly target
- Estimated number of gifts needed
- Progress percentage
- A future-state fundraising message
- A short donor call-to-action message

## Why This Exists

Most fundraisers fail because the goal feels too large, unclear, or emotionally heavy.

This free mini tool turns the fundraiser into a clear picture:

> “Here is the goal.  
> Here is what we already have.  
> Here is what remains.  
> Here is what we need each day.  
> Here is the message we can share.”

Once the numbers become clear, the stress begins to shrink.

The team can breathe again.

The path appears.

## The Belief Bubble

This app is built around a simple idea:

Before people act, they need to feel the future state.

They need to imagine the team funded, the children helped, the mission supported, the uniforms purchased, the trip paid for, the playground built, the church project moving forward, or the family receiving help.

The calculator helps turn raw numbers into a story people can believe in.
